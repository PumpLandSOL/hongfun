The rules · 规则

Rev. 2 · Robinhood Chain · Practice and Live ledgers · red is up

一Pairing

Every coin on the pad is launched against one underlying from the listed set: Tencent, Alibaba, BYD, Meituan, Xiaomi, NIO, Li Auto, JD.com, SMIC, ICBC, Ping An and PetroChina on HKEX; Kweichow Moutai, Ping An A and Wuliangye on Shanghai and Shenzhen; CATL on Shenzhen; PDD and the Alibaba ADR in the US. At launch the underlying's live print is recorded as the coin's index base. The base never changes.

二Price

curve_price = vUSDG / vTOK constant-product, virtual reserves 3,000 USDG · 1.073B tokens index = stock_now / stock_at_launch read every 15 s from the exchange tape price = curve_price × index buy: usdg_in × 0.99 / index → into the curve → tokens out by x·y = k sell: tokens in → curve → usdg_out × index × 0.99

Demand moves the curve. The stock moves the multiplier. A coin paired to BYD at 79.05 HKD trades at 1.10× its curve when BYD prints 86.96. The index P&L is absorbed by the pad's reserve.

三Sessions

HKEX 09:30–12:00 and 13:00–16:00 HKT. Shanghai and Shenzhen 09:30–11:30 and 13:00–15:00 CST. US 09:30–16:00 ET. Outside a session the underlying's last print holds, so the index is frozen and the coin trades on its curve alone. At the open the index jumps to the new print and every paired coin gaps with it. The board labels each name 开盘 (open), 午休 (lunch) or 收盘 (closed).

四Red envelopes · 红包

One percent of every coin's supply is set aside in 88 envelopes. The first 88 distinct buyers each open one on their first buy. The amount is a hash-seeded draw between 0.3× and 2× of an even split; every eighth buyer's envelope is multiplied by 1.88. Envelopes are credited on top of the purchase and appear on the coin's page and on the buyer's wallet. Envelope tokens are ordinary tokens.

五Fee & $HONG

Every buy and sell pays 1%. Half buys $HONG at market and burns it; with HONG_MINT and a treasury key set the burn executes each period; the board shows the USDG amount and the $HONG it buys at the DexScreener price. The other half funds the envelope pool of future launches. $HONG has no other function. It is not yield.

六Graduation · 毕业

When a coin's net raised reaches 8,888 USDG the curve closes and the coin is marked listed. The raised USDG and remaining supply seed a DEX pool at the closing price and the LP is locked; the coin is frozen at its last curve price and shown with the 上市 seal. Duplicate tickers are banned across the pad forever.

七Ledgers · Practice and Live

  • Practice. Every wallet receives 1,000 USDG on connect. It trades the practice board only.
  • Live. Funded with USDG (0x5fc5360D0400a0Fd4f2af552ADD042D716F1d168, 6 decimals) sent on Robinhood Chain to the treasury address shown on the board. The server reads the transaction receipt and requires a USDG Transfer event from your wallet to the treasury; the credited amount is the event's value. Minimum 10 USDG. Each hash is credited once and kept on the ledger with its block.
  • Boards. Coins carry the mode they were launched in. Live coins trade only with live USDG, practice coins only with practice USDG, so the two never mix.
  • Treasury. A wallet on Robinhood Chain, published on the board. Its USDG balance is read every 30 seconds via balanceOf and shown with the block number of the read.
  • Withdraw. Request any amount up to your free live balance. It leaves the ledger immediately and joins the payout queue with an id. The treasury pays the queue in USDG to the depositing wallet and records the payout transaction hash on the request.

九Risk

Coins are indexed to a stock, not shares of it. Holding a Moutai-coin gives no claim on Kweichow Moutai. A paired coin can lose value from the curve, from the underlying, or both. Use Practice to learn the pad before funding Live, and size positions you are comfortable holding.
