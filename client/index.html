规则How it works

Four rules. None of them are a treasury.

01 · Pair

Choose the underlying from 18 listed Chinese names across HKEX, Shanghai, Shenzhen and the US ADRs. The stock's live print is locked as your coin's index base at launch.

02 · Index

Price = curve price × (stock now ÷ stock at launch). Moutai up 3% since you listed? Your coin's whole curve is up 3%. Demand moves the curve, the tape moves the multiplier.

03 · Red envelope

1% of every coin's supply goes into 88 red envelopes. The first 88 buyers each open one: a random bonus, hash-seeded, and every 8th buyer gets 1.88× more. Lucky numbers are not a joke here.

04 · Fee

1% on every buy and sell. Half buys $HONG at market and burns it. Half fills the envelope pool for the next launches. At 8,888 USDG raised the coin graduates and lists.

05 · Sessions

Shanghai and Shenzhen trade 09:30–15:00 with a lunch break. Hong Kong to 16:00. When the exchange is closed the index freezes at the last print and the curve keeps trading. The board shows 开盘 / 午休 / 收盘.

06 · No custody

Ledgers are simulated with a paper USDG balance per wallet. Prices are real. Nothing here is a security, a yield, or advice. On-chain curves come with the contract.

问答Questions

Why pair a memecoin to a stock?

Because a coin that only reflects its own hype has nothing under it. Paired coins inherit a real tape. When BYD rips on delivery numbers, every BYD-coin rips with it, and the memes compete on top of that. It gives Chinese-market traders something to trade 24/7 in their own names.

Is it really Tencent?

No. It's a coin whose price is indexed to Tencent's live print. No shares, no dividends, no rights. The index is read from the exchange tape via the Yahoo chart API every 15 seconds.

What happens when the stock is closed?

The multiplier freezes at the last print. The curve still trades. When Shanghai opens at 09:30 CST the multiplier jumps to the new print and every paired coin gaps with it. Overnight positioning is the game.

What is $HONG for?

It's the token that half of every fee buys and burns. More launches, more trades, more burn. It has no other function.

Why red for up?

Because that's how the Shanghai, Shenzhen and Hong Kong exchanges display it, and because the pad is for them.
